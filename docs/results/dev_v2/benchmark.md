# Benchmark: before/after attacker view

Generated by `scripts/benchmark.py` (defaults, `harden=True`). No dopplertrack fit/search is run; success is judged by eye on the attacker's whitened spectrogram.

- **residual dB**: narrowband (≈2 Hz heterodyne) energy along the curves after/before (truth tracks for synth, detected for real). Noise-limited: it cannot go below -(tone SNR).
- **contrast**: mean attacker-dB on the curve minus beside it (±3–6 Hz). >+1 dB = ghost, <-1 dB = notch, ≈0 = gone. *smoothed* = 2 s time-averaged red-team view.
- **SNR outside**: orig vs. orig−clean outside ±3 Hz corridors; **speech SNR**: 300–3400 Hz.
- **AF** (anti-forensics, docs/research.md; `evaluate.anti_forensics`): **z** = significance of on-curve power over the per-bin floor integrated over every frame of the pass (null = same statistic on curves shifted ±8–14 Hz; z>3 = an integrating attacker sees it); **≤-10 dB?** = 95% upper bound of that residual is ≥10 dB under the floor (n/a when the curve was not detectable before cleaning: then the bound only reflects measurement precision); **edge** = signed step across the ±2 Hz corridor edges vs control curves (<0 notch, >0 over-fill); **var/kurt** = corridor-bin power variance vs flank (dB) and corridor excess kurtosis (noise ≈ 6); **leftover k·f** = harmonics of the true/detected fundamental with z>3 after cleaning.
- **box-free** (no truth, no boxes, independent of dopplerscrub's detector; `scripts/boxfree.py`): a simple attacker-side ridge extractor (2 s smoothed view > 4 dB, ≥ 6 s, sweeping ≥ 3 Hz) runs on the ORIGINAL 40–450 Hz view; **ridges z>3** = those an integrating attacker confirms, **still** = how many keep z>3 in the cleaned file, **(new)** = z>3 ridges found only in the cleaned file, split into **created** (an artefact of the processing), **unmasked** (original content inside the original's bright mask, separable once its neighbours were removed, power not raised) and **seen** (already z>3 along the same curve in the original); see docs/results/new_ridges.md. **persistent cells** = share of smoothed cells > 4 dB (white noise: 4.6%; 0 ridges).
- **event-box p99**: 99th percentile of whitened dB inside the annotated event boxes (airshow, colsto); pure noise ≈ 7 dB, so → ~7 means the ridges are gone.
- **visual verdict**: written after viewing each PNG (see `verdicts.json`).

_Verdict snapshot: Blind visual verdicts on the dev split at 37c5f34 (detector+remover integration: chirps.py fast-chirp tracker, per-track width_hz + skirt), written by the devrun session from the PNGs before any numbers were read; run 2026-11-04. colsto (full hour) not re-run (--no-colsto)._
